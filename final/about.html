About the School of Asian Languages and Cultures

About Us

Our Mission

The School of Asian Languages and Cultures is dedicated to connecting Asia with Latin America and the world through high-quality language education, intercultural collaboration, and academic research. We offer innovative programs in Japanese, Chinese, and Korean, preparing our students to thrive in a globalized society.

Our Values

  • Promoting multilingualism and cultural awareness
  • Building inclusive and diverse learning environments
  • Encouraging cross-cultural communication and understanding
  • Collaborating with international partners and communities

Our Vision

To become a global leader in Asian language education and cultural exchange, fostering meaningful connections between people, countries, and regions through the power of language.
