Partners and Community

Global Collaboration Through Language

Our school works closely with institutions, embassies, local governments, and cultural organizations in both Asia and Latin America. Together, we host cultural events, teacher exchanges, and language immersion programs that create meaningful impact.

We are proud to support community language programs and cultural heritage initiatives that preserve and promote Asian languages globally.

Embassy Collaborations

Partnering with Asian embassies to celebrate language and culture through public events and scholarships.

Academic Partnerships

Working with international universities to promote bilingual research and faculty exchanges.

Community Projects

Engaging with local communities through after-school programs and cultural festivals.
